# Lumi: a pitch to the NSW Office for Youth

*From a young person in Auburn, Cumberland (Sydney), November 2026*

## The problem

Young people in NSW are struggling, and many feel nobody is listening:

- The top issues for young people in 2026 are **mental health, cost of living and housing** (NSW Office for Youth, Youth Week 2026 polling report, 2,300+ respondents aged 10–24).
- **29%** of young people already use **AI** to support their mental health (same report).
- **76%** feel **unheard** in government decision-making (NSW Youth Summit 2024 report).

In places like Auburn there's another hidden load. In Cumberland, **73.9% of households** use a language other than English at home (ABS Census 2021). In Auburn North it's **83.7%**, compared with 12.4% in a typical Australian area. Many young people become their family's interpreter for letters, bills and appointments. Western Sydney University researchers call this **"hidden care"**. In their study, people started doing it at **age 11 on average**, some as young as 7. NSW guidance (Multicultural NSW) says children should never be used as interpreters, but nobody counts how often it still happens.

## The idea: Lumi

**Lumi** is one friendly app that brings together help young people usually have to find in many places. A 3D character makes it feel like a buddy, not a form.

1. **Talk to Lumi.** An AI companion you can talk to out loud. It shows helplines immediately when someone is at risk.
2. **Warm handover ("Tell it once").** Lumi turns your chat into a **Story Pass** you control, so you don't have to retell your story to every counsellor or GP. This builds on the fact that young people already turn to AI for support.
3. **Lumi Lens.** Snap a confusing letter or payslip. Lumi explains it and **translates it for your family**, which takes some of the weight off young language brokers.
4. **Calm, check-ins, money and work rights.** Breathing, worry shrinking, mood tracking, a personal price-rise calculator, and an unpaid trial check based on Fair Work guidance.
5. **My voice ("The Receipt").** Every idea a young person shares with government gets a **tracking code**, like a parcel, so they can see what happened to it. This responds directly to the 76% who feel unheard.

## Why it's different

- It's **one buddy for many problems** (feelings, money, work, family letters, having a say), not ten separate apps.
- It's **built for multicultural families**, with translation for parents.
- It works for **everyone**: Cloud AI, a free on-device AI that keeps everything private, or no AI at all.
- It's **private by default**: personal data stays on the young person's device.

## A 2027 pilot

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Jan–Feb | Co-design with young people at the Western Sydney Educate 2 Advocate session |
| 7–17 April | Launch during Youth Week 2027 events in Cumberland: schools, libraries, youth services |
| May–Aug | Test with real users. Professional translators check the translations. Track use (anonymously) |
| Sept | Share a "What we heard" report and feed it into the Strategic Plan for Children and Young People 2027–2030 |
